# Strike Hangars — Precinct Plans (⏳ proposed, 27 Nov 2026, awaiting Jordan)

Laid out 27 Nov 2026 following the Port of Arrivals pattern (`Port_of_Arrivals_Precincts.md`). Strike Hangars is the bow district of Deck 5 (Aerion), from the prow tip to F34, the full width of the deck. Canon (Sheet S5, errata C2 and C4): 42.1 sq mi (109.1 km², Rev B), racks for 3,600 craft and 32 launch shafts up to the Forward Grids, 60,000 people (pilots, deck crews, mechanics, armorers, launch controllers), centre address D5-F19-M, Keelway stop Strike Hangars at F3. The launch shafts are 197 × 131 ft (60 × 40 m) each, at F16–F28 in lanes D–F (port) and T–V (starboard). The precincts fill the district on the Rev B outline, which measures 42.23 sq mi (109.37 km²) forward of F34; errata C2's 109.1 km² is a rounded figure (flagged for Jordan). Everything here is a proposal unless marked canon. Plans are true top views: **bow right, port up**.

Layout rule: the bow end is the crews' way in (Keelway Gate), then the magazines (Ordnance Yards) kept away from everything else, then the four strike wings' rack halls either side of the Keelway with the launch shafts along both hull shoulders, and the repair yards and Wing Command at the stern end next to the Port of Arrivals and the Cruiser Docks. The craft mix in each rack hall (interceptors, strike, heavy strike, scouts) is an assumption: canon only gives the 3,600 total, here 4 wings × 6 squadrons × 150 racks.

Common sizes (proposed, as in the Port of Arrivals): main roads 131 ft (40 m), streets 98 ft (30 m), the Keelway corridor 328 ft (100 m). Every building is kept 98 ft (30 m) clear of the streets, so no block edge ever cuts a building. Building heights, floors and interiors are not designed yet.

Building shapes (Jordan, 27 Nov 2026): buildings follow their blocks. Each block's land, 98 ft (30 m) in from its streets, the hull and the plaza rims, is shared out between its buildings by their listed size, 66 ft (20 m) apart, so buildings take the block's curves along the Spine Atrium, the Atrium Plaza and the hull. Each building then gets a shape that suits its use: courtyard, L-shape, U-shape, curved front, rounded corners or oval; halls, hangars, yards and parks fill their lot. The Size column gives each building's overall size; its shape is in the notes. Map colours show what each building is used for (legend on the map).
