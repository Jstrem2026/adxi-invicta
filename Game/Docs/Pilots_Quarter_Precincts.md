# Pilots' Quarter — Precinct Plans (⏳ proposed, 27 Nov 2026, awaiting Jordan)

Laid out 27 Nov 2026 following the Port of Arrivals pattern (`Port_of_Arrivals_Precincts.md`). The Pilots' Quarter is the stern end of Deck 5, F59 to the stern wall, the full width of the deck. Canon (Sheet S5): 17.4 sq mi (45.0 km²), housing, simulators, flight school, 45,000 people (flight instructors, trainee pilots, shopkeepers, home helpers), centre address D5-F63-M, Keelway stop Pilots' Quarter at F65 (end of the line). Everything here is a proposal unless marked canon. Plans are true top views: **bow right, port up**.

Layout rule (Jordan, 26 Nov 2026): Aerion's homes, schools and family services sit here. The flight school and simulators face each other across the town centre (Pilots' Commons); the homes fill the rounded stern either side of the Keelway station. Home counts are not designed yet.

Common sizes (proposed, as in the Port of Arrivals): main roads 131 ft (40 m), streets 98 ft (30 m), the Keelway corridor 328 ft (100 m). Every building is kept 98 ft (30 m) clear of the streets, so no block edge ever cuts a building. Building heights, floors and interiors are not designed yet.
