# Flight Control — Precinct Plans (✅ canon, approved by Jordan)

Laid out 27 Nov 2026 following the Port of Arrivals pattern (`Port_of_Arrivals_Precincts.md`). Flight Control is the band across the whole deck from F55 to F59, between the Port of Arrivals and Cruiser Docks (bow) and the Pilots' Quarter (stern). Canon (Sheet S5): 7.7 sq mi (20.0 km²), the Beacon tower, traffic control, briefing halls, 15,000 people (controllers, flight officers, dispatchers, analysts), centre address D5-F57-M. The Keelway runs through the middle; the Atrium Plaza (canon) just touches the bow edge. Jordan approved this plan as canon. Plans are true top views: **bow right, port up**.

Layout rule: approach control and sensors at both hull ends, where the docks and gates are; briefings on the port side, dispatch on the starboard side, and the Beacon with the master traffic room in the middle beside the Keelway. The Beacon's height is not designed yet (errata D1 allows one landmark per city up to 1,181 ft / 360 m).

Common sizes (proposed, as in the Port of Arrivals): main roads 131 ft (40 m), streets 98 ft (30 m), the Keelway corridor 328 ft (100 m). Every building is kept 98 ft (30 m) clear of the streets, so no block edge ever cuts a building. Building heights, floors and interiors are not designed yet.

Building shapes (Jordan, 27 Nov 2026): buildings follow their blocks. Each block's land, 98 ft (30 m) in from its streets, the hull and the plaza rims, is shared out between its buildings by their listed size, 66 ft (20 m) apart, so buildings take the block's curves along the Spine Atrium, the Atrium Plaza and the hull. Each building then gets a shape that suits its use: courtyard, L-shape, U-shape, curved front, rounded corners or oval; halls, hangars, yards and parks fill their lot. The Size column gives each building's overall size; its shape is in the notes. Map colours show what each building is used for (legend on the map).

### P1 Port Approach

- **P1-01 Port Approach Control**: The approach control room for the port side. Controllers here guide every ship and shuttle coming in to the port docks and the Hall of Arrivals.

- **P1-03 Pilot Boat Station**: The base for pilot boats, small craft that fly out to meet big ships and guide them safely into their berths.
