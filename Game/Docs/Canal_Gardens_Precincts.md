# Canal Gardens — Precinct Plans (✅ canon, approved by Jordan 27 Nov 2026)

Started 27 Nov 2026. Precinct split by Kylee, from her sketch. Canal Gardens is on Deck 3 (Meridian), starboard of the Keelway, between the Grand Bazaar (bow) and the Arena District (stern). Canon (Sheet S3): 5.8 sq mi (15.0 km²), 20,000 people, waterways, restaurants and garden squares; centre address D3-F57-T. Everything here is a proposal. Plans are true top views: **bow right, port up**.

District edges, read from the Sheet S3 plan: bow edge 26.658 km from the prow (just stern of F53), stern edge 29.638 km (just stern of F59), port edge the centreline (Keelway side, next to the Neon Mile), starboard edge the net floor edge at 5.488 km. That is 9,777 × 18,005 ft (2.98 × 5.488 km). The canon Atrium Plaza circle (centre F52, radius 1.785 km) cuts the port-bow corner.

## What each building is for

Each building's purpose (Jordan asked for one for every building, 27 Nov 2026). These describe the use of each building as planned; they follow each building's own status.

### P1 Grand Canal

- **P1-01 Grand Canal**: The Grand Canal, the waterway at the centre of Canal Gardens. Boats carry people along it and the gardens, dining and promenades of the district face onto it.
- **P1-02 Grand Entrance Plaza**: The main entrance plaza to Canal Gardens, where visitors arrive and get their first view down the Grand Canal.
- **P1-03 Main Passenger Terminal**: The passenger terminal for Canal Gardens, where visitors arriving in the district start their journey along the canal.
- **P1-04 Gateway Gardens**: Gardens at the gateway to the district, greeting visitors as they come in from the terminal.
- **P1-05 Canal Operations**: The office that runs the Grand Canal: boat services, water levels, the bridges and canal maintenance.
- **P1-06 Welcome Gardens**: Gardens on the far bank at the district entrance, welcoming people as they cross the canal.
- **P1-07 Waterfront Dining**: Restaurants and cafés on the canal front, where people eat looking out over the water.
- **P1-08 Public Boat Stop**: A public boat stop where people get on and off the canal boats.
- **P1-09 Recreation Park**: A park for play and recreation beside the canal.
- **P1-10 Emergency Services**: The emergency services post for Canal Gardens, covering fire, rescue and first aid along the canal.
- **P1-11 Promenades**: Walkways along both banks of the canal, where people stroll between the gardens, dining and boat stops.
- **P1-12 Access Road A**: The service road along the bow edge of the district, keeping deliveries and vehicles away from the canal front.
- **P1-13 Access Road B**: The service road along the stern edge of the district.
- **P1-14 Gateway Bridge**: A footbridge over the canal by the entrance, linking the two banks.
- **P1-15 Garden Bridge**: A footbridge over the canal by the gardens, linking the two banks.
- **P1-16 Grand Canal (B2)**: The second stretch of the Grand Canal, running on through the Waterfront Quarter.
- **P1-17 Riverside Dining**: Riverside restaurants along the second stretch of the canal.
- **P1-18 Culinary Market**: A food market where people buy and taste food from across the ship.
- **P1-19 Waterfront Social Gardens**: Gardens beside the water for gatherings, picnics and events.
- **P1-20 Public Boat Stop (B2)**: A second public boat stop, serving the waterfront.
- **P1-21 Canal Terrace Gardens**: Terraced gardens stepping down to the canal.
- **P1-22 Waterfront Restaurants**: A row of restaurants on the waterfront.
- **P1-23 Fountain Square**: A square with a fountain, a meeting place on the waterfront.
- **P1-24 Sunset Terraces**: Terraces facing along the canal where people gather to watch the evening light.
- **P1-25 Promenades (B2)**: Walkways along both banks of the second stretch of the canal.
- **P1-26 Access Road A (B2)**: The service road along the bow edge of the waterfront stretch.
- **P1-27 Access Road B (B2)**: The service road along the stern edge of the waterfront stretch.
- **P1-28 Social Bridge**: A footbridge over the canal linking the social gardens and restaurants on either bank.
- **P1-29 Grand Canal (B3)**: The third stretch of the Grand Canal, carrying boats on through the Waterfront Quarter towards the Central Canal.
- **P1-30 Artisan Market**: A market where makers and craftspeople sell what they make: pottery, textiles, glass and woodwork.
- **P1-31 Cultural Courtyard**: An open courtyard for performances, exhibitions and cultural events, with the Heritage Bridge arriving at its canal side.
- **P1-32 Sculpture Gardens**: Gardens for walking among sculpture, with paths and benches beside the canal.
- **P1-33 Waterside Café Terraces**: Cafés with outdoor terraces facing the canal, for coffee and light meals.
- **P1-34 Canal Amphitheatre**: An outdoor amphitheatre by the canal for concerts, plays and public events.
- **P1-35 Reflection Gardens**: Quiet gardens for sitting and resting, laid out to look over the water.
- **P1-36 Floating Market Landing**: The landing where market boats tie up and sell from the water to people on the bank.
- **P1-37 Waterfront Picnic Park**: A park with lawns and tables for families and groups to picnic beside the canal.
- **P1-38 Promenades (B3)**: Walkways along both banks of the third stretch of the canal.
- **P1-39 Access Road A (B3)**: The service road along the bow edge of the waterfront stretch.
- **P1-40 Access Road B (B3)**: The service road along the stern edge of the waterfront stretch.
- **P1-41 Heritage Bridge**: A footbridge over the canal linking the Cultural Courtyard and the Reflection Gardens.
- **P1-42 Grand Canal (B4)**: The Central Canal, the widest stretch of the Grand Canal. It opens into a 180 m basin for boats to turn and gather, with plazas, dining and gardens all round it.
- **P1-43 Canal View Gardens**: Gardens on the port bank where B3 meets the Central Canal, looking along the water.
- **P1-44 Waterfront Gallery Quarter**: A quarter for galleries and cultural spaces beside the canal, with the Gallery Bridge arriving at its canal side.
- **P1-45 Grand Canal Plaza**: The great public plaza on the port side of the basin, for crowds, events and views across the water.
- **P1-46 Central Dining Quarter**: Restaurants and cafés at the south end of the basin on the port bank, where people eat looking over the water.
- **P1-47 Canal Transit Landing**: The port-bank landing where people join and leave the canal boats and change to other transport.
- **P1-48 Ornamental Gardens**: Formal ornamental gardens on the stern bank where B3 meets the Central Canal.
- **P1-49 Waterfront Pavilion Quarter**: Dining pavilions and small eateries on the stern bank of the canal.
- **P1-50 Grand Fountain Gardens**: Large gardens with fountains on the stern side of the basin.
- **P1-51 Festival Terraces**: Stepped terraces on the stern side of the basin where crowds gather for festivals and events.
- **P1-52 Waterfront Recreation Park**: A park for play and recreation at the south end of the basin on the stern bank.
- **P1-53 Public Boat Landing**: The stern-bank landing where people get on and off the canal boats.
- **P1-54 Promenades (B4)**: Walkways along both banks of the Central Canal and around the basin, where people stroll between the plazas, gardens and landings.
- **P1-55 Access Road A (B4)**: The service road along the bow edge of the Central Canal block.
- **P1-56 Access Road B (B4)**: The service road along the stern edge of the Central Canal block.
- **P1-57 Gallery Bridge**: A footbridge over the canal linking the Waterfront Gallery Quarter with the gardens and pavilions on the far bank.
- **P1-58 Central Bridge**: A footbridge over the canal at the south end of the basin, linking the Central Dining Quarter and the Waterfront Recreation Park.
- **P1-59 Grand Canal (B5)**: The fifth stretch of the Grand Canal, carrying boats on from the Central Canal Basin through the garden waterfront towards Garden Reach.
- **P1-60 Waterfront Tea Gardens**: Tea gardens and small tea houses on the port bank, where people sit by the water.
- **P1-61 Wellness & Spa Quarter**: A quarter for spas, baths and wellness, where people come to rest and relax beside the canal.
- **P1-62 Water Lily Gardens**: Quiet gardens with lily ponds for walking and sitting, with the Serenity Bridge arriving at the canal side.
- **P1-63 Public Boat Stop (B5)**: A public boat stop on the port bank where people get on and off the canal boats.
- **P1-64 Ornamental Water Gardens**: Formal gardens of pools and fountains on the stern bank.
- **P1-65 Fine Dining Terraces**: Fine restaurants on terraces facing the canal.
- **P1-66 Serenity Square**: A calm public square on the stern bank, with the Serenity Bridge arriving at its canal side.
- **P1-67 Garden Gateway Park**: A park at the gateway to Garden Reach, welcoming people on towards the next block.
- **P1-68 Promenades (B5)**: Walkways along both banks of the fifth stretch of the canal.
- **P1-69 Access Road A (B5)**: The service road along the bow edge of the Central Canal II block.
- **P1-70 Access Road B (B5)**: The service road along the stern edge of the Central Canal II block.
- **P1-71 Serenity Bridge**: A footbridge over the canal linking the Water Lily Gardens and Serenity Square.
