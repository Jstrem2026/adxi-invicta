Structural requirement (derived). Each sky column is 40 m across (1,257 m²) and the bible places one per km² of deck. At the keel the 190 columns of Deck 8 plus the 50 m Atrium wall (251,327 m²) have 490,088 m² of section. If they alone carried everything above the ventral armor (382 billion tonnes) at 1 g, the average compressive stress would be 7.6 GPa. In practice the five keel girders, the ring frames and the armor shell carry most of it, so the real column stress is lower, but the design still needs a structural material rated at about 23 GPa compressive for the columns and wall at a safety factor of 3. No known material does this; the ship's Void Ore composite is specified to meet it (a fictional material, stated as a requirement). No dimension needs to change.

8 · CONSISTENCY

Consistency ledger. (1) Deck sizes and net floor areas are fixed canon; each deck outline is the hull planform inset 120 m on the flanks and scaled to the deck's width, and the gap to the stated floor area is a perimeter service ring of 81 to 538 m. (2) The "950 km³ enclosed" figure is the interior volume. With armor the whole hull is about 1,100 km³. (3) The mass is computed bottom-up from volumes and densities as 530 billion tonnes and is canon at about 530; the earlier 143 billion tonne estimate could not hold the armor, which alone would exceed it. (4) Every district area on the deck sheets is solved to the hundredth of a km², and the sum of the eight decks is exactly 1,950 km² and 1,820,000 people.
