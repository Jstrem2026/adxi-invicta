# Canal Gardens — Precinct Plans (✅ canon, approved by Jordan 27 Nov 2026)

Started 27 Nov 2026. Precinct split by Kylee, from her sketch. Canal Gardens is on Deck 3 (Meridian), starboard of the Keelway, between the Grand Bazaar (bow) and the Arena District (stern). Canon (Sheet S3): 5.8 sq mi (15.0 km²), 20,000 people, waterways, restaurants and garden squares; centre address D3-F57-T. Everything here is a proposal. Plans are true top views: **bow right, port up**.

District edges, read from the Sheet S3 plan: bow edge 26.658 km from the prow (just stern of F53), stern edge 29.638 km (just stern of F59), port edge the centreline (Keelway side, next to the Neon Mile), starboard edge the net floor edge at 5.488 km. That is 9,777 × 18,005 ft (2.98 × 5.488 km). The canon Atrium Plaza circle (centre F52, radius 1.785 km) cuts the port-bow corner.

## Layout

Three bands, bow to stern. Lines are set so each precinct has exactly the area in Kylee's sketch. Because the Atrium Plaza takes 1.36 km² out of the bow band's port corner, P4 runs deeper across the beam than the sketch shows.
